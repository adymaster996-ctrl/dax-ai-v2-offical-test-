GET STARTED

Connect Dax AI

Dax AI is powered by Google Gemini. Your API key stays on your server, not in the browser.

  1. Get a free Gemini key at Google AI Studio.
  2. Copy .env.example to .env and add your key.
  3. Run npm install, then npm start, and open http://localhost:3000.
For a public launch, add authentication, rate limiting, budget limits, and privacy terms before sharing the app.
